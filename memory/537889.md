## Verdict and variable/medium reasoning

- No claim. The article varies, between its two syntheses, the laser wavelength (532 vs 1064 nm),
  the irradiance (17 vs 71 GW/cm2) and the ablation time (3 vs 10 min), but these changes are
  confounded with the ablated metal (Ag vs Au), the lens (4 vs 5 cm), the water volume (3 vs 25 ml)
  and the vessel, and no sentence states that the size of the NPs changes with fluence, energy,
  power or intensity. Each metal is produced at one fixed setting and gets one size value.
- The only laser-parameter dependence the text states is on the ablation time (p.3), which is not
  fluence, energy, power or intensity under claims_spec §5, and it is stated for fragmentation and
  concentration, not for size.
- The irradiance values are intensity (GW/cm2) but appear only as fixed synthesis settings and in a
  threshold mechanism sentence; no size dependence on irradiance is stated anywhere.
- `medium_class` was not needed: no claim row was written. For the record, both colloids are metal
  NPs in ultrapure Milli-Q water (p.2), i.e. `water` under results_spec §2.

## Rejected sentences and reasons

- p.3 "The optimum laser ablation time was then chosen in order to obtain the highest possible
  concentration with the highest reproducible size of AgNPs." — the no-claim quote: it names size
  and a varied laser parameter (ablation time), but ablation time is not fluence, energy, power or
  intensity, and the sentence states a selection criterion, not a size dependence.
- p.3 "It is important to underline that increasing the laser ablation time improves the
  fragmentation [24] and increases the concentration." — dependence on ablation time, and on
  fragmentation/concentration, not on size.
- p.3 "if the concentration of the colloidal solution increases over a certain value, the Debye
  length decreases [25] ... thus increasing the probability that NPs enlarge their size and form
  clusters or aggregates." — concentration effect on size, not a laser variable.
- p.2 "If the laser irradiance overcomes the breakdown threshold ... a plasma is generated." —
  mechanism/threshold, no size dependence (claims_spec §2 excludes mechanism explanations that
  state no dependence).
- p.2 "The NPs size was determined by TEM and SPR absorption spectroscopy and resulted to be
  10 ± 3 and 15 ± 4 nm for AgNPs and AuNPs, respectively." — single size per metal at one setting;
  the Ag/Au difference is a material difference, not a laser-variable dependence.
- p.3 "the resonance wavelength of the SPR peak ... is related to the size of the NPs [20]" and the
  p.4 Haiss correlation — size measurement method, not a laser variable.
- p.6 phosphate "slight size quenching effect ... as reported in ref. [27]" — ionic strength and
  stabilizer, not a laser variable.
- p.7 "the larger size (lesser curvature) and surface area of bare AuNPs as compared to AgNPs" —
  material comparison, no laser variable.
- p.6-7 aggregation/precipitation kinetics, λSPR peak2 shifts, TTR and E16V results — aggregate
  dimensions and protein behaviour, not the size of the ablated NPs versus a laser variable.
- Fig. 1-10 captions and axis labels — data and labels, not claims.
