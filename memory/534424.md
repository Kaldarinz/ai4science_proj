## Verdict: two own-data claims
Energy series (50, 100, 200, 500, 1000 mJ; p.6, "By varying the laser energy ..."):
- `534424-c1` — increase, low-energy region 50–100 mJ: p.6 run ending "... In this region, the average
  size of gold nanoparticles begins to increase 55." The variable is carried by "At these low energies"
  (start of the same sentence run), so the minimal consecutive run was quoted, not the last sentence alone.
- `534424-c2` — decrease, high-energy region 200–1000 mJ: p.6 "So, for high energies, as the energy
  increase, the size of the gold nanoparticles decreases only slightly."
Both: variable `energy` (text calls it "laser energy"/"laser power", units mJ → energy, claims_spec §5),
material Au (GA-AuNPs), medium_class `aqueous` (10 mL DI water + 20 mg Gum Arabic).

## Rejected sentences and why
- p.1, p.6, p.9 pulse-number statements ("Increasing the number of pulses has only a minor effect ...",
  "no significant effect on nanoparticle size", "influences the nanoparticle size slightly and remains
  less than 3 nm") — the varied laser variable is the number of pulses, not fluence/energy/power/intensity.
- p.9 "Laser ablation parameters have a huge impact on GA-AuNPs' properties." — "properties", not size, and
  the article varies pulses *and* energy, so the §2 "only one of the four variables" condition fails.
- p.9 "The laser energy also influences GA-AuNPs' qualities. Energies above 200 mJ provide great stability
  with small NPs sizes." — restatement of `c2` (same material/medium/variable/direction/basis), later
  occurrence; quote taken from Results (p.6), not duplicated.
- p.1 "For energies greater than 200 mJ, there is a blue shift toward shorter wavelengths" and p.5–6
  absorption-peak shifts — spectral property, no size change stated.
- p.5 GA-concentration and p.6 AAS concentration statements — concentration, not size; medium variable.
- p.4 sizes at GA = 0 and GA = 20 mg (16 ± 9 nm, 1.85 ± 0.99 nm) — medium comparison, not a laser variable.

## Numerical/matching decisions (after extraction)
- Series `534424-s1`: TEM geometric diameter, particle-count weighting, mean, 2 points (200 mJ, 1000 mJ),
  medium `aqueous`, 1064 nm; shape `decreasing` under both models.
- `c1`/`s1`: include, `unassessable` — series has no point inside 50–100 mJ (the 50/100 mJ experiments are
  `short_series` exclusions: 537045, 537090) and the text reports no size at those energies; comparing
  against 200–1000 mJ would test a different range.
- `c2`/`s1`: include, `direct` — both points inside the claimed high-energy region; agreement true in both
  models. Caveats recorded: two points only; histogram means from bar centres (4.97, 1.89 nm) vs text
  Gaussian fits (5.10 ± 3.97, 1.85 ± 0.99 nm); errors are the 10%/20% fallbacks, stored SDs wider.
