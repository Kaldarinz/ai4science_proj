# 545860 — Gentile et al., J. Nanopart. Res. 23 (2021) 35

Gold nanoparticles obtained by ns-pulsed laser ablation in liquids (ns-PLAL) are arranged in the form of
fractal clusters. One main PDF (`10.1007_s11051-021-05140-5.pdf`, 12 pp), no supplement.

## Verdict

No claim. One `no_claims.csv` row quotes the p.4 sentence about bimodal distributions depending on laser
power and ablation time.

## Why the p.4 sentence is not a claim

1. It states that bimodal size distributions of PLAL-produced NPs are often reported and that "this depends
   also on the laser power and on the ablation time". The subject is the occurrence of a bimodal distribution,
   not a change of nanoparticle size with a laser variable, and no direction is given. The same reading was
   applied to 537967, where "bimodal size particle distributions are formed if high fluences are used" was
   rejected for exactly this reason.
2. Material: the sentence says "NPs produced by PLAL" with no gold or gold-containing material named, so
   claims_spec §2's material condition is not met (the same ground on which 537967 rejected its generic
   cited sentences).
3. Basis would be `cited` (Sportelli et al. 2018), never own data: the article's own experiments use one
   fixed fluence of 64 J/cm² (p.9) and vary only the ablation time and the KCl concentration.

## Variable / medium notes (for the curator)

- The only laser value in the whole text is the single fluence 64 J/cm² (p.9). A keyword sweep over the
  normalized pages finds "fluence" only there; "power" only in the p.4 cited parenthetical and in "power law"
  (p.4, p.5); "energy" only as plasma/internal energy and Born energy (p.2, p.8); "intensity" only as
  scattered/spectral intensity; no "irradiance", no mJ, µJ, W/cm² or GW/cm² value anywhere.
- The article's own medium is Milli-Q water with KCl 100 µM, i.e. `aqueous` under results_spec §2 (water with
  an additive). This matters only if the curator decides the p.4 sentence is a claim; then the medium of the
  cited statement is unstated and `unspecified` would be the honest label.

## Rejected sentences and reasons

- p.3: "the size distribution of np prepared by plal is known to depend on the ion concentration of the
  ablation solution (merk et al. 2014)" — ion concentration, not a laser variable.
- p.2: "nps can grow until the equilibrium between np evaporation and condensation is reached" and "other
  particles, with variable shape and size, can be formed at the border of the plasma, during the exchange of
  energy between the plasma and the surrounding water" — mechanism, plasma energy, no laser variable.
- p.4: "the probability of secondary ablation is non-negligible, and this can lead to the fragmentation of
  primary particles in smaller ones" — mechanism, no laser variable and no varied setting.
- p.9: "the np concentration increases with the ablation time until eventually reaching a steady value of
  16 nm because of the increasing concentration of aunps within the laser radiation path, thus producing an
  attenuation of the incident beam (yang 2012)" — ablation time and concentration, not fluence/energy/power/
  intensity; the attenuated beam is a mechanism, not a stated size-versus-fluence dependence.
- p.9–p.10: "the average np size, evaluated over more than 50 syntheses, is reproducible within 20% when
  evaluated by dls and by 10% when evaluated from the spr" — reproducibility, not a laser variable.
- p.1 abstract (7.6 nm primary particles, d = 1.9), p.8 (11% vs 14% fractions), p.9 conclusions (rp = 3.2 nm,
  ξ ~ 13 nm, 85% of the mass) — single-setting structural results, no laser variable.
- All figure captions (Figs. 1–5) and axis labels — excluded by claims_spec §2.
