## Rejected sentences and reasons

1. p.2 "Ultrashort laser ablation (femtosecond (fs) or picoseconds (ps))
   has demonstrated the most efficient control of NPs among PLAL methods
   (see examples in [33-35])." — recorded as the no-claim quote: the named
   laser property is pulse duration, not fluence/energy/power/intensity;
   "control of NPs" is not size specifically; no direction; cited.
2. p.3 "As previously demonstrated [46,47], the addition of NaCl to water
   improves colloidal stability of laser-synthesized Au NPs and provides
   in situ control of the mean size of Au NPs by adjustment of NaCl
   concentration." — size control by NaCl concentration, not a laser
   variable; cited.
3. p.3 "During the first step we synthesized Au NPs with mean size 7.5 nm
   using radiation from the Yb:KGW laser (420 fs, 1025 nm, 10 kHz, 50uJ)
   ..." — a single size at one fixed energy; data, not a claim.
4. p.3 "During the second step ... laser ablation was performed with the
   same laser, but with slightly modified parameters (repetition rate
   5 kHz, pulse energy 100 uJ) for 15 minutes." — different target and
   step, no size stated per setting, no dependence.
5. p.2 "Such techniques provide relatively good control of size,
   composition, and structure of the Fe@Au core-satellites" — chemical
   routes, not laser.
6. p.2 "Barcikowski et al. ablated Au-Fe alloy target and found that
   structure of the produced core-shell NPs depends on NPs size and nature
   of the liquid media [40,41]" — structure vs NP size and medium, not a
   laser variable; cited.
7. p.5 "It should be noted that the core-shells nanostructures with Au core
   and Fe-based shell were also formed (data not shown), when large
   (>10 nm) Au NPs were present in the colloid during the ablation of Fe
   target." — Au NP size affects structure, not a laser variable.
8. p.9 "Second, the satellites in the nanocomposites are predominantly
   formed by the smallest Au NPs presented in initial gold colloid." —
   size selection during formation, not a laser variable.
9. p.9 "The second observation can be rationalized by higher speed of NPs
   Brownian motion of smaller NPs (the speed depends on the size as r-3)."
   — mechanism, no laser variable.
10. p.9 "Moreover, note that in our laser ablative setup some of already
    formed nanostructures are re-irradiated by the laser beam." — no size
    dependence stated.
11. Fig. 2c caption (p.6) and Fig. 4a caption (p.8) — captions alone,
    excluded by claims_spec section 2.
12. SI Table S1/S2 — DLS size vs incubation time and coating, not a laser
    variable.

## Decisions

- No claim: the article varies no laser parameter to study size. It uses
  one fixed Au synthesis setting (50 uJ, 10 kHz) and one fixed Fe step
  (100 uJ, 5 kHz), reports single mean sizes (Au satellites 7.5 nm, Fe
  cores 30 nm), and attributes size control to NaCl concentration, not to
  fluence/energy/power/intensity.
- One no_claims row, quote from p.2 (the closest sentence).
- Reading attestation `complete`, bound to the two-PDF hash.
