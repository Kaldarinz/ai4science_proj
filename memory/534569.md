# Article 534569 — Laser ablation-based one-step generation and bio-functionalization of gold nanoparticles conjugated with aptamers

## Files and pages
- `10.1186_1477-3155-8-21.pdf` (main, 11 pages)

## Size–fluence results
No claims found. The article does not study the dependence of nanoparticle size on laser fluence/energy.

- Laser parameters (page 8, Methods): pulse energy fixed at 100 μJ, repetition rate 5 kHz, fs pulses (120 fs, 800 nm).
- "laser fluence was optimized in regard to maximal productivity, while avoiding degradation of the oligonucleotide" — optimization criteria, not a size–fluence relationship.
- The paper studies the effect of **aptamer concentration** on nanoparticle size (pages 3–4), not fluence.

## Material and medium
- Material: Au (gold)
- Medium: Tris buffer (50 mM, pH 8.0) for miniStrep aptamer; HEPES buffer (20 mM, pH 7.4) for anti-PSMA aptamer
- Medium class: colloid (buffer solution)

## Rejected sentences
- Page 4: "On first sight, the AuNPs hydrodynamic diameter increase seems to be contradictory to our previous finding of a growth quenching effect induced by increasing DNA concentrations[19]." — discusses DNA concentration, not fluence.
