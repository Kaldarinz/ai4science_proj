## Verdict

One own-data claim, `539228-c1` (p.4): the Au NP size on GO depends on the laser fluence; direction not stated
(`affects`). Material Au, medium `aqueous` (water with graphene oxide as the added component; no feedstock colloid).

## Variable decision

`fluence`: the four varied settings are stated in J/cm2, called "laser energy densities" on p.3 and "laser fluences"
on p.4, and "13.2 Jcm−2 laser energy" on p.8. Unit is J/cm2, so claims_spec §5 gives `fluence` regardless of the
word "energy". No pulse energy in mJ/J, no power in W and no intensity in W/cm2 is stated anywhere.

## Claim set decision (why one claim, not two or three)

- p.2→p.3: "…the purity, structure, size and stability of the AuNPs were fine-tuned by different laser parameters
  like laser fluence, repletion rate, laser wavelength, irradiation time, etc." The sentence is split by the page
  break, so it cannot be quoted within one page under claims_spec §4, and its content is the same dependence as the
  recorded results sentence. Not recorded separately.
- p.11: "The tuning of laser parameters influences the size and concentration of the AuNPs." Same dependence, broader
  wording ("laser parameters" instead of "laser fluence"), no direction. claims_spec §2 makes a dependence restated in
  results and conclusions one claim, quoted from the results section, so it is folded into c1.
- Curator question: if the project prefers the broader wording as a distinct claim (variable `unspecified`,
  basis `own_data`, direction `affects`), the p.11 row would have to be added as `539228-c2`. I did not add it because
  it would double-count one experiment and one assertion.

## Medium label correction (disclosure)

The claim was first written with `medium_class=aqueous` (water with graphene oxide as an added component).
`prepare` then produced zero candidates, because the article's series 539228-s1 is classified `colloid` in the
snapshot: each experiment lists a feedstock colloid (541112, sheet-shaped particle 535424, the GO suspension) at
fraction 1.0 in a deionized-water medium (liquid_media 541110 -> liquid 530032 -> base_liquid 120001 "water").
Re-reading results_spec §2 against the text, the medium is "an aqueous suspension of the GO solution" (p.3), i.e. a
pre-existing colloidal dispersion fed into the ablation vessel, so "contains a feedstock colloid" applies and
`colloid` is the first applicable label, ahead of `aqueous` (water with dissolved additives). The claim row was
changed to `colloid` and re-committed. Disclosure for the curator: the change was made after candidate generation
and after the series metadata and its shapes had been seen; the argument for it is textual (suspension = colloid,
not a dissolved additive), not agreement-driven, and it changes no extraction judgment other than the label.
Curator question: if the project treats the GO suspension as an additive rather than a feedstock colloid, the label
should go back to `aqueous`, the claim then has no candidate and its outcome is `no_candidates`/unassessable.

- The article states four fluences but never reports a size per fluence: the only size value is "~30 nm" on p.5 with no
  fluence attribution, and the TEM figures are not resolved per setting. The claimed dependence therefore has no tabulated
  size data in the text.
