## Verdict: no claim (one no_claims row)
The article is a methodology paper: laser-synthesized Si NPs (37 ± 3 nm) and Au NPs (4 ± 0.8 nm) are
chemically assembled into Si@Au core-satellites; the studied variables are APTMS amount, water/ethanol
ratio and Si:Au volume ratio, and the measured response is the plasmonic peak position (520 → 560–610 nm).
- No fluence, energy, power or intensity is varied or even reported: single laser setting
  (Yb:KGW, 1025 nm, 10 kHz, 480 fs, p.3). The words "fluence"/"energy"/"power"/"intensity" occur only
  in the reference list (p.11–12).
- One mean size per material, no size series at any laser setting.
- `no_claims` quote: p.2 "As an example, we recently elaborated the technique of femtosecond (fs) laser
  ablation in water and organic media, which renders possible an efficient control of size characteristics
  of NPs from a variety of materials, including Au [22,25], Si [26,27], TiN [28,29] NPs." — closest to a
  claim (technique → size control) but names no laser variable, gives no direction, and the §2 condition
  ("of the laser parameters, the article varies only fluence/energy/power/intensity") fails.

## Rejected sentences and why
- p.2 "the first laser-ablative approach typically leads to a wide size and shape dispersion of formed
  composites" — dispersion of the co-ablation route, no laser variable, no size-vs-laser dependence.
- p.2 "The required red-shift of the plasmonic peak could be reached at smaller core-shell sizes if a high
  refractive index material such as silicon (Si) is used a core instead of silica (SiO2) [13,14]" —
  size → optical property, material choice, not a laser variable.
- p.6 "Si NPs solutions mixed with 80 µL APTMS were stable without any change of size characteristics of
  Si NPs" — APTMS amount, not a laser variable.
- p.7 "the mean size of silicon core was 37 nm silicon, while the thickness of Au NPs-based shell was about
  4 nm" — single values, no laser variable.
- p.8 "the resonance feature can be tuned dramatically toward the NIR region while the Si-core size
  increases up to 60 nm" — Mie simulation, size → optical property.
- p.9 "the dissolution rate of these NPs can be varied from several days to several weeks by changing
  parameters of the preparation procedure [26]" — dissolution rate, preparation procedure, not size.
- p.10 conclusions ("Several parameters such as APTMS concentration and type of synthesis medium were
  optimized to obtain stable Si@Au nanocomposites having a relatively small size (less than 40 nm)") —
  chemical parameters, not laser variables.
- Figure axis labels/captions (Figures 1–6: size distributions, extinction spectra) — excluded by §2.
