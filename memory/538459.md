## Verdict: three claims

- `538459-c1` (main p.6, `affects`, `fluence`, `own_data`): "The NP size was dependent on
  laser settings and was optimized for a fluence of 30 J s −1 leading to a NP size of 13 nm."
  A dependence statement with no direction. On 2026-11-17, human curator Anton corrected
  `variable` from `unspecified` to `fluence`: the sentence explicitly names fluence, and SI p.3
  describes the varied setting in J/cm². Preserve the literal quote, including its unit typo,
  and `variable_written="laser settings"`. The article varies fluence (30 and 40 J/cm², SI p.3);
  variation of the medium additives is allowed by claims_spec §2.
- `538459-c2` (SI p.3, `decrease`, `fluence`, `own_data`): "By trend, core diameter decreases
  with pulse power." The authors' own first tests, Fig. S2.
- `538459-c3` (SI p.5, `affects`, `energy`, `general`): "Normally, their size and size
  distribution depend on the lifetime of the bubble and hence on the laser pulse energy and
  liquid properties." Stated as common PLAL knowledge ("Normally"), no citation marker in the
  sentence, so `general` rather than `cited` (claims_spec §6).

Why the main-text methods sentence and the SI p.3 "varied with laser settings" sentence are not
separate claims: both share the curator-corrected tuple `(Au@SiO2, aqueous, fluence, affects, own_data)` with
`538459-c1`, and claims_spec §2 allows one claim per distinct tuple, quoting the results section
when it states the dependence. The main p.6 sentence is in Results and discussion and is
self-contained, so it is the quote; the SI p.3 sentence is the underlying own-data evidence and is
recorded in `conditions`.

## Variable decision for `538459-c2` (textual support)

The sentence says "pulse power" with no unit. claims_spec §5 decides the variable by the unit of
the quantity the text varies, not by its name. The same experiment is described two sentences
later: "Since we encountered some problems concerning the stability of the glass vessel at
40 J·cm⁻², we chose to continue with 30 J·cm⁻² and 1·10⁴ M sodium silicate concentration", and
Fig. S2 is captioned "after synthesis with different laser pulses and sodium silicate
concentration". The varied quantity is therefore a fluence in J/cm², recorded as `fluence` with
`variable_written="pulse power"`. The main text is internally inconsistent about the same setting:
p.3 writes "laser fluence of 30 J cm −1" (J/cm, not J/cm²) and p.6 writes "a fluence of 30 J s −1"
(J/s = W, i.e. a power unit). Neither typo changes the recorded variable, because the SI states
the unit of the varied quantity as J·cm⁻².

## Medium decision

`aqueous` for `538459-c1` and `538459-c2`: the synthesis medium is distilled water with two
additives (0.4 µM NaCl and 0.4 µM NaSiO2, main p.3), so results_spec §2 gives `aqueous` (water
with additives), not `water`. `538459-c3` is a general PLAL mechanism statement about NP growth in
any liquid ("and liquid properties"), so its medium is `unspecified`; it is a `general` claim and
therefore gets no audit or comparison rows.

## Textual inconsistencies (for the curator, not extraction decisions)

- The same nominal setting is written "30 J cm −1" (main p.3), "30 J s −1" (main p.6) and
  "30 J·cm⁻²" (SI p.3, Fig. S3 caption). Only the SI form is dimensionally a fluence.
- Main p.3 states an average diameter of 9 nm for the as-synthesised NPs, while main p.6, Table 1
  and SI p.4 report 13.0 nm for the same material; SI p.3 also gives a 9 nm mean core diameter.
  The 9 nm values appear to refer to the earlier small-NP tests (SI p.3 warns that those data used
  a significantly smaller number of NPs), but the main text does not say so.
- SI p.3 says the coating thickness "did not depend on soluble glass concentration but rather on
  laser settings", while the Fig. S2 caption says shell thickness "hardly varies with sodium
  silicate concentration" — consistent, but the caption's "By trend, core diameter decreases with
  pulse power" is the only quantitative direction given, and no fluence values are printed for
  the individual S2 points in the text.
- Main p.10 says ICP-MS was used for the organ contents, while the methods (p.6) specify ICP-OES.
