## Verdict: no claim, one no_claims row
- The synthesis uses one fixed laser configuration, p.2: "in compliance with the following terms: 1064 nm basic
  wavelength, power 4 w, pulse frequency 10 hz, and the width of the pulse is 6 nm." (the "6 nm" is evidently a
  typo for 6 ns; the laser is called "nanosecond pulsed"). No fluence, energy, power or intensity is varied
  anywhere; the only varied quantity is the AuNP doping level of the PEO/CMC films (10, 15, 20, 25 wt.%, Table 1).
- One size value at that single setting, p.7: "tem micrograph shows the formation of nano-size gold with a
  diameter ranging between 10 and 25 nm" and "15e20 nm nanoparticles size is the dominant size"; the abstract
  (p.1) and conclusions (p.7) repeat "10 and 25 nm". No sentence states that size changes, or does not change,
  with a laser variable, so claims_spec §2 gives no claim and §8 requires one no-claim row.
- The no-claim quote is the two-sentence run p.7 "Fig. 8 illustrates TEM/histogram micrographs of synthesized gold
  nanoparticles (AuNPs) using a laser ablation route. TEM micrograph shows the formation of nano-size gold with a
  diameter ranging between 10 and 25 nm." — it links the laser-ablation route to the reported size, i.e. the
  closest the text comes to a size-versus-laser statement.

  electrooptics, inc.)." and the settings sentence — a fixed configuration, no size and no dependence.
- p.1 abstract "x-ray diffraction analysis and tem micrograph shows the formation of fcc crystal structure of
  nano-sized gold with a diameter ranging between 10 and 25 nm." — a single size value, no laser variable.
- p.7 "the distribution of nanoparticles shown in the histogram curve reveals that 15e20 nm nanoparticles size is
  the dominant size." — a single distribution, no laser variable.
- p.7 conclusions "both xrd and transmission electron microscopy approve the formation of gold nanoparticles in

  the single size value.
- p.4 "such sharp intense band can be attributed to the surface plasmon resonance (spr) of gold nanoparticles

- p.2 "surface plasmon resonance (spr) can be observed in the spectrum of developed nanoparticles and attributed
