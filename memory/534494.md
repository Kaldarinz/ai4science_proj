# 534494 — In situ decoration of graphene sheets with gold nanoparticles (Sci. Rep. 2016)

## Verdict (revised after the SI arrived)
One own-data claim: `534494-c1`, SI p.2 — changing the fluence modifies the particle
size. The earlier no-claim row (main text only, commit 6f14a51) was withdrawn: the
supplied SI was absent from that reading and it states the dependence explicitly.

## Evidence
- SI p.2, section 1 ("Particles created in GO + H2O using a fluence of ~0.25 J/cm2"):
  "The histogram shows that changing the fluence value modifies the particle size and
  the size distribution." Followed by "Using a fluence value of ~0.25 J/cm2 the average
  size of the gold nanoparticles is 6 nm." Own experiment, Fig. S1, 434 particles.
  Direction is not stated in the sentence → `affects`; unit J/cm² → `fluence`;
  medium deionized water, no additives → `water`.
- Main text p.2: water at ~1 J/cm² gives 26 nm (Fig. 2b, 324 particles).
- Main text p.6: the ~0.25 J/cm² water experiment gives 6 nm, compared with 3 nm in GO.

## Rejected sentences and why
- Main p.6 "The modulation of laser properties and the presence of the graphene material
  have an influence in the size of the AuNPs." — restates the same dependence in the
  discussion/conclusion; per claims_spec §2 the results statement (SI p.2) is quoted and
  this is not a second claim. Recording it separately as `variable=unspecified` would
  double-count one dependence in two prevalence buckets.
- Main p.2 "The physical processes are generally governed by the laser radiation power,
  the pulse duration, the repetition rate..." — mechanism, no size dependence stated.
- Main p.5 "GO acts as a neutral density filter avoiding the delivery of the full input
  irradiation fluence ~1 J/cm2..." — mechanism for the medium effect, no size–fluence
  dependence stated.
- Main p.6 "This value is significantly different from the average value obtained using
  graphene oxide of 3 nm..." — comparison of reported values (data, not a claim); the
  media differ as well.
- SI p.2 "Different studies demonstrate that using energies below 65 µJ the normal
  average size for the produced particles is in the range of 7-10 nm [1]." — cited single
  size range at one energy range; no dependence stated.
- SI p.4 "We observed that there is not a significant change in the size of the AuNPs
  when modifying the graphene concentration." — GO concentration, not a laser variable.
- SI p.5 "the influence of graphene oxide in the Au-NPs synthesis is the promotion of
  lowering the average particle size" — medium effect, not a laser variable.

## Variable / medium / material decisions
- material: Au (AuNPs).
- medium: deionized water (claim medium `water`); the GO suspension (1 mg/mL) is the
  second medium of the article but no laser parameter is varied in it.
- Laser parameters: 800 nm, 30 fs, 1 kHz fixed; only fluence varies (~1 vs ~0.25 J/cm²),
  the second value produced with a neutral density filter replacing the GO absorption.
