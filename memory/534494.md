# 534494 — In situ decoration of graphene sheets with gold nanoparticles (Sci. Rep. 2016)

## Verdict
No size–fluence/energy/power/intensity claim. Row added to `results/no_claims.csv`.

## Why no claim
The article's independent variable is the **medium** (deionized water vs graphene
oxide suspension), not a laser parameter. Fluence is fixed at ~1 J/cm² in both
main experiments (water: 26 nm; GO: 3 nm). The size difference is attributed to
GO acting as a neutral density filter and capping agent, not to varying fluence.

The closest sentence is on p.6 (Discussion): "The modulation of laser properties
and the presence of the graphene material have an influence in the size of the
AuNPs." It names "laser properties" but the article does not vary any laser
parameter (only the medium), so per claims_spec §2 it is not a claim.

## Rejected sentences and why
- p.2 "The physical processes are generally governed by the laser radiation power,
  the pulse duration, the repetition rate..." — mechanism explanation, no stated
  size dependence.
- p.5-6 complementary experiment: water at ~0.25 J/cm² gives 6 nm vs 26 nm at
  1 J/cm² — a comparison of reported values (data, not a claim); also the medium
  differs (no GO), and it tests the GO-as-filter hypothesis, not a fluence claim.

## Variable / medium / material decisions
- material: Au (AuNPs).
- medium: water (no additives) and aqueous GO suspension (1 mg/mL).
- No laser variable is varied; fluence fixed ~1 J/cm² (main), 0.25 J/cm² (complementary).
