# Article 534494 — In situ decoration of graphene sheets with gold nanoparticles synthetized by pulsed laser ablation in liquids

## Files and pages
- `10.1038_srep30478.pdf` (main, 9 pages)

## Size–fluence results
One claim (density filter experiment):
- Water at ~1 J/cm² → 26 nm (page 2, Fig. 2b).
- Water at ~0.25 J/cm² → 6 nm (page 6, density filter experiment).
- Claim: size increases with fluence (own data, Au, water).

The article's main finding is that graphene oxide reduces the fluence reaching the gold disk (density filter effect) and acts as a capping agent, producing smaller AuNPs (3 nm in GO vs 26 nm in water). The density filter experiment (water at 0.25 J/cm² → 6 nm) is the size–fluence dependence.

## Material and medium
- Material: Au (gold)
- Medium: deionized water (for the density filter experiment); graphene oxide suspension in water (for the main GO experiment)
- Medium class: water (for the density filter claim)
- Conditions: fs pulses (30 fs, 800 nm, 1 kHz, max 0.8 mJ/pulse)

## Rejected sentences
- Page 6: "The modulation of laser properties and the presence of the graphene material have an influence in the size of the AuNPs." — general statement about laser properties; restatement of the density filter dependence, not a separate claim.
- Page 1 (abstract): "Moreover, the presence of graphene oxide influences the size of the nanoparticles..." — about GO's effect, not fluence.
- Page 6: "The results show that for all concentrations of graphene oxide the size of the gold nanoparticles is in the range of 2–5 nm." — about GO concentration, not fluence.
