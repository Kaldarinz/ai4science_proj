## Size–fluence results (data, not claims)
The size–fluence dependence is established only by comparing two values:
- Water at ~1 J/cm² → 26 nm (page 2, Fig. 2b).
- Water at ~0.25 J/cm² → 6 nm (page 6, density filter experiment).

No sentence states that size changes with fluence; the dependence is reader-inferred
from the two values. Per claims_spec §2, this is data, not a claim. The article has a
`no_claims.csv` row (page 6, the 6 nm sentence).

- Medium: deionized water (for the density filter experiment); graphene oxide suspension in water (for the main GO experiment)
- Medium class: water (for the density filter experiment)
- Conditions: fs pulses (30 fs, 800 nm, 1 kHz, max 0.8 mJ/pulse)
