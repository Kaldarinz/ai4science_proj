## Verdict and variable/medium reasoning

- No claim. The article varies two things: the laser wavelength (355/532/1064 nm at a fixed
  15.5 J/cm2) and the duration of post-ablation illumination (0-25 min at a fixed 40 mJ). Neither
  is fluence, energy, power or intensity (claims_spec §5), so no size-versus-laser-variable claim
  exists.
- The one sentence that varies fluence (p.3, ref. 33) is about the solid-phase structure — "produce
  NPs instead of chains" — i.e. aggregation state and morphology, not size. The article separates
  size from shape in its own framing (abstract p.1, conclusions p.4) and its own comparison of the
  chain histogram (Fig. 1b) with the post-illumination histogram (Fig. 3) reports only a slight
  shift to smaller sizes (p.4), so the fluence effect it cites is not a size effect.
- `medium_class` was not needed: no claim row was written. For the record, all colloids are Au in
  double distilled water (p.2), i.e. `water` under results_spec §2.

## Rejected sentences and reasons

- p.3 "Generally, the size of the Au NPs rises and the particles size distribution becomes broader
  as the laser wavelength is increased" (and its restatement in the p.4 conclusions, and the p.1
  abstract/introduction framing): the varied quantity is wavelength, not fluence, energy, power or
  intensity.
- p.3 "The blue shift of the maximum of their SPE bands ... reflects the significantly greater mean
  particle size in the latter case": wavelength comparison, and an inference from spectra.
- p.3 "It means that the concentration of the colloids created by different wavelengths but equal
  laser energy and duration of ablation differ significantly": concentration, not size; energy is
  stated as equal.
- p.3 "Our previous study [33] has shown that it is possible to produce NPs instead of chains ... at
  different values of the laser fluence": structure/morphology, not size (see above). This is the
  no-claim quote because it is the closest — a fluence change with no size stated (claims_spec §8).
- p.3 "building of nanowires was not observed (or their number was much lower) because of the use of
  considerably higher laser fluence values": nanowire formation, not size; cited to [31,35].
- p.4 "Raising the duration of illumination ... shifts the size distribution to the smaller NPs" and
  the cited "shift being proportional to the irradiation duration": size, but the varied quantity is
  illumination time, not a laser pulse variable.
- p.4 "the disintegration of the chains is accompanied by a decrease of the mean size of their
  constituent particles": chain disintegration, no laser variable.
- p.4 "formation of chains necessitates higher local energy density in the colloid. It is provided
  by a focused incident laser beam": a mechanism statement about chain formation, no size
  dependence (claims_spec §2 excludes mechanism explanations that state no dependence).
- p.1 "points to a way to modify them using illumination by unfocused laser beams of different pulse
  energy": "modify" is not a size statement, no direction, and the actual experiment holds the pulse
  energy at 40 mJ and varies the duration.
- p.2 "the size of the laser beam spot was about 6 mm": beam spot, not particle size.
- p.1 "The small size of the nanoparticles allows for sufficient mobility by Brownian motion":
  background, no laser variable.
- p.2 "the size reduction of Au NPs by laser light irradiation [24]" and "utilized for size
  modification of the Au NPs [25-29]": literature framing with no fluence/energy/power/intensity
  value and no stated dependence.
- Fig. 1-4 captions and the TEM histograms: axis labels and data, not claims.

## Questions for the curator

- Borderline judgment: the p.3 ref. 33 sentence is the only place where a fluence change is tied to
  a change in the product. I read "produce NPs instead of chains" as structure/morphology, not size,
  because the article separates size from shape and its own Fig. 1b/Fig. 3 histograms differ only
  slightly. If the curator reads chains-versus-NPs as a size statement, this becomes one `cited`
  claim (`variable=fluence`, direction `affects`, `material=Au`, `medium_class=water`) and the
  no-claim row must be replaced; it would still get no audit or comparison row as a cited claim.
