## Verdict: no claim

The article varies **no laser fluence, energy, power or intensity during ablation**: Sec. 2 (p.2) gives one
setting (800 nm, 200 ps, 1 kHz, ~10 J/cm2, fixed 10 min irradiation). One mean size (30 nm, 10-90 nm
range, p.2) is reported for that setting. The article's actual variables are physical ones, not laser ones:

- delay between the heating nanosecond pulses and the driving femtosecond pulses (200-1000 ns, Sec. 3.2),
  reported against **harmonic yield**, not against NP size;
- Z-scan/TA probe/pump energies (30 nJ probe, 52 nJ pump) at fixed settings for **optical nonlinearities**;
- target type (bulk Au vs Au NP film) for harmonic efficiency.

None of these states a size change with fluence/energy/power/intensity.
