## Verdict: no claim

The article varies no laser parameter. The synthesis is one fixed setting (Nd:YAG 1064 nm,
7 ns, 10 Hz, 60 mJ/pulse, 2000 pulses, p.2; repeated in the Fig. 1 caption, p.3) and the only
varied factor is the POSS-thiol concentration of the ethanol medium (EtOH, POSS 70% = 0.0168 mM,
POSS 100% = 0.024 mM, p.2). The reported size differences follow that medium composition
(8.7 ± 0.1, 10.1 ± 0.1, 8.9 ± 0.1 nm, p.5, Table 1) and the colloid stability, not a fluence,
energy, power or intensity change.

Recorded no-claim quote (p.2): "This method also, offers the ability to produce colloids with
spherical geometry, whose sizes and productivity can be modified with the irradiation
parameters [26]." It is the closest sentence because it links size to laser irradiation
parameters, but it is cited to ref. [26], states no direction, and the article varies no laser
parameter at all, so the claims_spec section 2 condition for an unnamed "laser parameters"
dependence (only fluence/energy/power/intensity varied) is not met.

## Rejected sentences and reasons

- p.6 conclusions "Moreover, in the case of Au NPs radius, size can be tuned by changing LAL
  parameters, improving even more the biocompatibility of the nanocomposite." — names "LAL
  parameters", which include the liquid medium (the parameter this article actually varies), not
  laser parameters; no citation, no direction, and no fluence/energy/power/intensity is varied.
- p.5 "The first laser pulse travels through a stationary and homogenous media, where only the
  presence of solution components change the impinging fluence." — states that the solution
  changes the impinging fluence; no size statement, and the fluence is not a varied input.
- p.5 "Subsequent laser pulses suffer energy loses due to additional scattering and absorption
  from Au NPs formed." — mechanism of energy loss, no size dependence.
- p.5 "For our laser ablation parameters, more than 98% of the Au NPs radius was below 60 nm."
  — one fixed setting, single size statement, no dependence.
- p.5 "For EtOH, P 70% and P 100% as liquid media, we found a mean particle radius of 8.7 ± 0.1
  nm, 10.1 ± 0.1 nm and 8.9 ± 0.1 nm, respectively." and Table 1 — data on the medium
  composition; a dependence the reader infers from values is data, not a claim (section 2).
- p.5 "The formation of wide and/or bimodal distribution is a problem of great interest to be
  solved, where this phenomenon is attributed to complex factors such as target thermal heating
  by the plasma and bubble-induced mechanical erosion [31]." — mechanism, no laser variable.
- p.5 "In comparison with the EtOH colloid, P 70% exhibit a minor redshift and higher absorbance
  mainly due to the change in Au NPs size [8,33]" — size change attributed to the medium, cited,
  and the statement is about the plasmon band.
- p.5 "It has been reported that the monodispersing state of the Au NPs in the colloid also
  affects the absorbance spectra ..." and the chain-array two-band explanation [8] — optical
  property, mechanism, no size-versus-laser statement.
- p.6 "Since the same ablation parameters were used for each synthesis (same wavelength, pulse
  number, material, focusing, etc.) ..." — explicitly holds the laser fixed.
- p.6 "POSS concentration in the solution influenced the mean radius and the concentration of
  the Au NPs suspended in the colloid." — POSS concentration, not a laser variable.
- p.6 "The cytotoxicity observed can be related mainly to the influence of ethanol in HGF cells
  [34] and the smaller Au NPs [35]." — cytotoxicity, not size versus laser.
- p.1 abstract "TEM characterization showed spherical geometry of the Au NPs, mainly smaller
  than 60 nm." — single size value at one setting.
- p.2 "To study the effect of the initial amount of POSS-thiol in the final Au NP colloids we
  used 3 liquid media ..." — announces a medium study, no laser variable.
- Figure captions (Figs. 1-4) — captions alone are excluded by section 2.
