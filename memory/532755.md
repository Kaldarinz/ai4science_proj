# 532755 — Svirshchevskaya et al., Int. J. Mol. Sci. 2022, 23, 2134

Silicon–Gold Nanoparticles Affect Wharton's Jelly Phenotype and Secretome during
Tri-Lineage Differentiation. Main PDF 19 pp, supplied SI 4 pp.

## Coverage / evidence locations

- Main p.1: title, abstract (NP types SiNPs, AuNPs, SiAuNPs made by laser ablation;
  all biological endpoints).
- Main p.2 §2.1 "Characterization of NPs": PLAL synthesis statement (refs 27–29),
  rotated targets, composition of SiNPs/SiAuNPs, STEM of one SiAuNP, sizes
  "SiNPs and SiAuNPs were 148 ± 37 and 185 ± 40 nm (Figure 1e,f)", "AuNP diameter
  ... approximately 20–40 nm (Figure 1d)".
- Main pp.3–12: MSC phenotype, cytotoxicity/ROS, Luminex secretome results and
  discussion (Figures 1–8); no laser variable.
- Main pp.13–14 §4.1–4.3 synthesis: Yb:KGW, 1030 nm, 270 fs, "up to 400 µJ pulse
  energy, 1–100 kHz" (laser capability spec, p.13); Si target in 14 mL ultrapure
  water, 30 min, 2 mm × 2 mm scan at 5 mm/s, 75 mm lens, 10 mm liquid layer;
  Au target (99.99%) in 7 mL aqueous 0.1% NaCl, 2 mm/s target movement; SiAuNPs =
  second ablation of the SiNP suspension over a gold target.
- Main p.14 §4.4 characterization: STEM, EDS, Raman, DLS (661 nm, 90°).
- Main pp.15–19: MSC methods, statistics, supplementary list, author statements,
  references.
- SI pp.1–4: Table S1 (41-plex factor list), Figs. S1–S5 (flow cytometry, factor
  concentrations, time dependence, total concentrations, SiNP effect). No synthesis
  parameters, no size values.

## Verdict: no claim

The article reports one fixed synthesis condition per NP type and single size
values; it varies no laser parameter at all, so no sentence can state a size change
with fluence, energy, power or intensity, and the claims_spec §2 condition for an
unnamed-"laser parameters" claim (only fluence/energy/power/intensity varied) is not
met.

Keyword sweep over the whole main PDF: "fluence" only inside "confluence" (p.14)
and "influence" (pp.11, 13); "power" only the Raman "power on the sample was 1 MW"
(p.14); "intensity" only "mean fluorescence intensity" (MFI, p.5); "energy" only the
laser spec "up to 400 µJ pulse energy" (p.13) and "electron energy of 20 eV" (p.14).
No J/cm², mJ or µJ value describes a varied ablation dose. The SI contains none of
these words.

## Rejected sentences and reasons

- p.2 "NPs were obtained by the method of pulsed laser ablation in liquid (PLAL)
  [27–29]." — method statement, no size dependence, cited.
- p.2 "Earlier, we modified PLAL using solid silicon and gold targets [28]." —
  method provenance, no size statement.
- p.2 "The targets were rotated during PLAL in order to ensure homogenous ablation
  (Figure 1a)." — states a purpose for target rotation, not a size-vs-laser-variable
  dependence; no size value attached.
- p.2 "The concentration of Si and Au in NPs was estimated by the targets weighing
  before and after the ablation." — concentration measurement, not size.
- p.2 sizes "148 ± 37 and 185 ± 40 nm" / "approximately 20–40 nm" — single values at
  one fixed synthesis condition; a reader could not infer any fluence/energy trend,
  and the sentence states no dependence. Kept as the closest-to-claim quote of the
  no-claim row.
- p.13 laser spec "up to 400 µJ pulse energy, 1–100 kHz" — instrument capability
  range, no size value and no dependence stated; the experiments use one setting.
- p.14 "Position of the focusing lens was adjusted to obtain maximum productivity of
  the ablation process, as measured by weighting the target before and after the
  ablation." — optimisation of productivity (mass yield), not of size; focusing is a
  laser parameter that is not fluence/energy/power/intensity.
- p.14 "To ensure a homogenous ablation process, the ablation target was continuously
  moved with 2 mm/s speed" — scanning, not a laser dose variable; no size statement.
- p.14 Raman "power on the sample was 1 MW" — characterisation excitation, unrelated
  to NP size.
- p.5 "MFI designates mean fluorescence intensity." — fluorescence, not laser.

## Notes / questions for the curator

- The AuNP size is not measured ("We were unable to measure the AuNP diameter");
  SiNPs and SiAuNPs have DLS means only. Even if the snapshot holds an experiment for
  this article, no own-data laser-variable series can be claimed from the text.
- Medium: AuNPs are ablated in 0.1% NaCl aqueous solution (additive), SiNPs in
  ultrapure water, SiAuNPs in the SiNP suspension (feedstock colloid). Irrelevant to
  the no-claim verdict but recorded for consistency.
