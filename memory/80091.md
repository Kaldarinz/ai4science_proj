## Verdict: no claim (one no_claims row)
- The article is an in vivo safety/biodistribution/pharmacokinetics study of one dextran-coated AuNP batch.
  It varies no laser parameter and reports one mean size: "particles prepared in dextran solution had the
  mean size of 21 nm and size dispersion less than 10 nm full-width-at-half maximum" (p.3, Fig. 1B).
- Methods p.9 gives the laser as a capability range, not a varied series: "we used a Yb:KGW laser (Amplitude
  Systems, 1025 nm, 480 fs, 100-200 uJ per pulse, 1-100 kHz)". No size is reported per energy or per
  repetition rate, and no synthesis condition is varied.
- Keyword check over the whole PDF: "fluence" occurs only in the reference list (p.11); "energy", "power"
  and "intensity" do not occur anywhere; "pulse" occurs in the body only in the Fig. 1 caption (p.3) and the
  Methods laser sentence (p.9). So no sentence in the body can state a size dependence on one of the four
  variables.
- Closest sentence, quoted in `results/no_claims.csv` (p.2): "Since this method does not depend on the
  presence of specific chemical products (e.g., gold salts) during the synthesis, it can avoid any
  contamination of nanoparticle surface, while the use of ultrashort (femtosecond) laser ablation enables to
  finely control size and structural characteristics of formed AuNP40,41,43-45." It names pulse duration
  (ultrashort/femtosecond), not fluence/energy/power/intensity, gives no direction, and is cited
  (refs 40,41,43-45). Same shape of sentence as the 532464 no-claim row.

   used for the reported batch. I read it as the laser's capability range, not as a varied energy series,
   because no size is reported per setting. Confirm that this does not constitute an energy variation for
   claim purposes.
2. The p.2 sentence attributes size control to femtosecond pulse duration. Pulse duration is not one of the
   four variables of claims_spec section 2, so it is not a claim; if the curator intends pulse duration to
   count as an "unnamed laser parameter", the verdict would change. Recorded as no claim.
