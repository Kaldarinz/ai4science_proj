## Verdict

One own-data claim, `538584-c1`: size **decreases** with increasing laser pulse energy, Au in
deionized water (`water`), quote from the results section p.4.

## Variable decision (energy vs fluence)

The article states the same four settings twice: as laser energies in mJ (setup p.2, Table 1, Table 3,
Table 5) and as the corresponding fluences in J/cm2 (setup p.2, Table 5). `variable=energy` is recorded
because the quantity the text varies and tabulates is the pulse energy in mJ; the fluence values are
introduced as a conversion ("which corresponded to the laser fluences of ..."). This follows
claims_spec §5 (decide by the unit of the varied quantity, not by its name). The choice does not change
any agreement: the four settings map one-to-one and monotonically between mJ and J/cm2, and the
numerical series uses fluence as x. Curator question: if the project prefers the fluence label for a
setting stated in both units, the row should be relabelled `fluence` with `variable_written` unchanged.

## Quote form (extraction artefact)

This Elsevier PDF extracts justified body lines without inter-word spaces (pypdf). The quoted sentence
therefore appears in the page text as
`DensitiesofAu-NPswereincreasedandsizesweredecreased (Table3)withtheraiseinlaserpulseenergywhichareconsistent withtheUV–Visresults(Fig.2)andotherliteraturereports[32].`
The quote is copied in that exact form because claims_spec §4 allows only whitespace collapsing, not
insertion of spaces. Readable rendering for the curator: "Densities of Au-NPs were increased and sizes
were decreased (Table 3) with the raise in laser pulse energy which are consistent with the UV–Vis
results (Fig. 2) and other literature reports [32]." No other sentence in the article states the
size–energy dependence with intact spacing, so no readable alternative exists.

## Textual inconsistencies for the curator

- p.2 setup states repetition rate 1 Hz for the first batch, while the second batch varies 2–8 Hz and
  Table 5 lists 1 Hz for the present work; the first-batch rate is consistent, but the abstract's
  "varied laser energies and repetition rates" does not say the two batches differ in fixed settings.
- p.4 text says the TEM mean size "was ranged from 5 to 30 nm" for the energy series (Table 3 gives
  7.5–30.1 nm) and "5 to 50 nm" for the repetition-rate series (Table 4 gives 10.5–17.5 nm); the
  abstract says 7–30 nm and Table 5 says 7–30 nm.
- p.2 "pulse reputation rate" (typo) and p.6 Table 2 header "reputation rate".
- p.4 Table 3 standard deviations are printed without separators ("±9.42102", "±5.98150"), so the SD
  digits are ambiguous (9.42 vs 9.42102).
- p.4 text calls 318 mJ the "optimum laser energy" for the TEM figure, while p.2 calls 96.6 mJ "the
  optimum energy level" that "produced the best NPs morphology in the first batch"; p.4 also calls 2 Hz
  the optimum repetition rate although Table 4's smallest size is at 8 Hz (10.49 nm).
- p.4 "The lattice spacing ... was found to be 0.119 nm" for Fig. 5(b), while Table 3 lists 0.119 nm for
  S4 (318 mJ) and 0.215 nm for S1; p.4 later states 0.2 nm for the repetition-rate sample (Table 4: 0.200
  for 2 Hz).
