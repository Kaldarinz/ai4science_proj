## Verdict

No claim. One `no_claims.csv` row quotes the p.2 sentence about the working parameters.

## Why the p.2 sentence is not a claim

1. The sentence names the parameters it is about: "1064 nm wavelength, 20 Hz repetition rate, and 60 fs pulse
   duration" versus "800 nm wavelength, 1 kHz repetition rate, 50 fs pulse duration". None of these is fluence,
   energy, power or intensity under claims_spec §5, so the stated dependence is not on one of the four
   variables. The same reading was applied to 537967 (repetition rate) and 537818 (wavelength).
2. The article varies none of these parameters: it runs one fixed setting (p.5), so the sentence is a
   comparison with a cited work, not a statement about a varied laser variable of its own.
3. "are all known to affect" is general knowledge with no own data and no direction ("differently" is not a
   direction), so even under a broader reading the basis would be `general` and the direction `affects`.

## Rejected sentences and reasons

- p.2: "thanks to a relatively inefficient fabrication from the energetic point of view" — a yield remark, no
  size dependence and no laser variable.
- p.2: "The poly-dispersity index was 2.9." — a single measured value at the one fixed setting.
- p.2: "The peak also shows no significant broadening and red-shift, which confirms the single size population
  of the NPs." — an optical property at one setting, not a size-versus-laser dependence.
- p.4: "As visible in the colors of Figure 2b, an effect of the NPs' size on the surface potential also
  appears, as the effect of the measured surface potential is additive." — the dependence runs from size to
  surface potential, the reverse of the claim direction, and names no laser variable.
- p.4: "the above-demonstrated surface oxidation, in agreement with the work by Sylvestre et al. [31], even
  though partial, is sufficient to prevent excess agglomeration above the 40 nm NPs size" — agglomeration
  threshold, cited, no laser variable.
- p.5: "Pulse energy was fixed at 10 mJ." and "The irradiation time was fixed at 10 min." — explicitly
  constant, so no dependence can be stated.
- p.5 conclusions (oxide coating, ligand-free NPs, extension to Ag and Cu) — no laser variable and no size
  dependence.
- All figure captions (Figs. 1–3) and axis labels — excluded by claims_spec §2.
