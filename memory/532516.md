## Verdict: no claim (one no_claims row)
The article varies **no** laser parameter. Table 1 (p.4) gives one setting for both liquids: energy 100 µJ,
wavelength 1030 nm, power 1000 mW, repetition rate 10 kHz, total duration 40 min (water) / 30 min (NaCl).
The reported size results are the evolution of the DLS size distribution with time after ablation
(1 h … 21/30 days) and the difference between deionized water and 10 µM NaCl. No fluence/intensity word
occurs anywhere in the text; "energy" occurs only in the scope sentence and in Table 1.

Closest-to-claim sentence (p.4, quoted in `results/no_claims.csv`): "Next, a systematic study was conducted
of the dependence of their dimensional, morph ological properties on the parameters of ablating laser
radiation: energy, duration of the synthesis process; as well as the type of liquid ... and the time after
synthesis ...". It announces the scope of the study, not a size–laser-variable result, and the article
varies none of the four variables, so the claims_spec §2 condition for a "laser parameters" dependence
fails. Note the internal inconsistency: the sentence promises an energy dependence that Table 1 and the
results never deliver (single energy, no size-vs-energy values).
