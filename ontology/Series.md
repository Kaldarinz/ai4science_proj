# Series

Possibly overlapping experiments of one article with pairwise-compatible exported fixed conditions and exact size quantity, technique, weighting, component and normalized location statistic, varying fluence or energy; missing condition details are wildcards, absent collections mean none, and spot diameter is ignored (results_spec section 2)

Relations from this class: reportedIn, computedBy.
Attributes: medium_class (colloid, water, aqueous, organic, other, unspecified); shape_primary (flat, increasing, decreasing, peak, valley, irregular); shape_lenient (flat, increasing, decreasing, peak, valley, irregular).
